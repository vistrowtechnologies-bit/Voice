## Calling window

TRAI norms limit unsolicited calls to 9am–9pm, Monday–Saturday. With **Enforce calling window** on, Vistrow Voice blocks outbound dials automatically outside the start/end time and allowed days you configure, evaluated in the callee's local timezone.

## Consent, recording & retention

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Honor Do-Not-Call list** | Never dials a number on your DNC registry. |
| **Require spoken consent** | The agent opens every call with a consent line and logs the caller's reply. |
| **Record calls** | Stores call audio alongside transcripts. |
| **Data retention (days)** | Transcripts and recordings older than this are purged automatically. `0` keeps everything indefinitely. |

<Tip>
  If you're subject to India's DPDP Act or a similar data-minimization requirement, set a real retention window here rather than leaving it at `0` — see your [Privacy Policy](https://vistrowvoice.com/privacy) for how Vistrow Voice itself handles retention and deletion requests.
</Tip>

## Do-Not-Call registry

Add numbers one at a time with **Block**, or use **Bulk import numbers** for a list. Every outbound dial — from a campaign or a one-off test call — is scrubbed against this list first.
