## Each integration's page

Every integration page has the same layout, with four tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Connection status, last delivery, and delivered / failed / skipped counts for the last 7 days. |
| **Settings** | What gets sent and which agents send here (see below). |
| **Activity** | A log of every delivery from calls and tests, with the reason when one fails. History is kept for 30 days. |
| **Setup help** | Step-by-step setup and fixes for common problems. |

The buttons at the top of the page:

- **Connect** — connect the integration (for example **Connect with Zoho**, or sign in with Google).
- **Send test** — sends a sample lead so you can check it arrives.
- **Reconnect** — re-authorise or pick a different destination, for example a new Slack channel.
- **Disconnect** — stop sending. You're asked to confirm first.

### Choose what gets sent

On the **Settings** tab:

- **What gets sent** — pick the moments that send: **End of call**, **Lead details during the call**, **Appointment booked**, and **Callback requested**.
- **Fields to send** (**Columns in your sheet** for Google Sheets) — for CRM / Webhook, Slack and Google Sheets, choose which fields go out, such as name, phone, email, details and summary, transcript, and a recording link. Anything you leave out is never sent.
- **Agents that send here** — turn an agent off to keep its calls out of this integration. Applies from its next call.

You can also choose integrations from the agent's side: open the agent, go to **After the call → CRM routing**.

## Google Sheets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open **Integrations → Google Sheets** and connect with your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your sheet">
    Vistrow Voice creates a spreadsheet called **Vistrow Voice — Leads** in your Google Drive. Each agent gets its own tab, named after the agent, with one row per caller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Send test** — a row marked "TEST — safe to delete" appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Callers who left no phone number or email are skipped on purpose; you'll see them as skipped on the **Activity** tab. If the sheet is deleted or Google access is removed, click **Reconnect**.

## ArthaLeads: call new leads automatically

On the ArthaLeads page's **Settings** tab, the **ArthaLeads leads and calls** section lets your agent call new ArthaLeads leads as soon as they arrive. Choose which lead sources to call, add a rule for each website page or project, and pick the **agent** and the **number** to call from. Then click **Save call setup**.

Only sources you turn on, with a matching page or project rule, are called. Do-Not-Call and calling-window rules still apply.

## How delivery works

After a call, the agent sends its result to every connected integration (or only the ones chosen in that agent's **CRM routing**), in addition to any webhook URL set directly on the agent under **After the call → Webhook**. Every lead carries the agent's 10-digit **Agent ID** as `agent_id`.
