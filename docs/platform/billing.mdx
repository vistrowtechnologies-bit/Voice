Vistrow Voice runs on credits. On the default setup — the **Vistrow Bharat** model with an **HD** or **Standard** voice — **1 credit = 1 minute** of conversation.

That rate is the same on every channel: website widget calls, browser calls and phone calls all use 1 credit a minute and draw from the same monthly pool. Telephony (your phone provider's call and number charges) is billed separately by that provider, not in credits.

<Tip>
  Every voice you can add on the [Voices](/platform/voices) page today is an HD or Standard voice, so every one of them runs at 1 credit a minute with Vistrow Bharat.
</Tip>

## Plans

Every plan includes the website widget, call recording, post-call data extraction, live call transfer, custom functions, caller memory, calendar booking, the Testing Lab, and call history and analytics.

You can see your current plan and credits under **Settings → Plan & billing**.

## Test calls

- **Dashboard test calls** — the **Browser test** and **Call test** buttons on an agent card — are free for the first **30 minutes each month**. Minutes past that are charged at the normal rate.
- **Testing Lab** runs are billed from the first second, like a real call.

## See where your credits go

The [Usage](/platform/usage) page (**Monitor → Usage**) breaks your credits down by day, channel, agent, voice and model, lists your most expensive calls, and shows how many credits you have left this cycle.
