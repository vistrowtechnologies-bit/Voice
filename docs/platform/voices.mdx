The **Voices** page (under **Build** in the sidebar) is where you listen to every voice and choose which ones your agents can use. Only voices you add here show up in an agent's **Voice** picker.

## The voice families

| Family | How many | Credits | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HD** | 30 | 1 credit/min | The fastest voices. Each one keeps the same persona across 52 languages — including Hindi, English and 9 other Indian languages — and switches mid-call when the caller does. |
| **Standard** | 10 | 1 credit/min | Natural, conversational voices for the 11 Indian languages. |

Credit rates assume the default **Vistrow Bharat** model. See [Billing](/platform/billing).

<Tip>
  Not sure which to pick? Start with an HD voice. New workspaces begin with a few voices already added, including **Myra HD** and **Kiara HD**.
</Tip>

## Preview and add a voice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a voice">
    Pick the **HD** or **Standard** tab, or use **Search all voices by name or language**. Narrow the list with the filters: **All**, **Added**, **Female** or **Male**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listen">
    Click any row to play its sample. Click it again to pause. Use the **Hindi** / **English** switch to hear the sample in either language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add to your picker**. The voice now appears in the **Voice** dropdown on every agent's **Voice & language** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take a voice out of the picker, select it and click **Added to your picker** — it changes to **Remove from picker** when you hover over it. Removing a voice doesn't change agents that already use it.

<Note>
  The **Added** filter shows every voice in your picker, across both tabs.
</Note>
