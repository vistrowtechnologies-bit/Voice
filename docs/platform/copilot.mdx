Click the **Artha** button in the bottom-right corner of any dashboard page. **Artha Copilot** reads your live workspace — calls, leads, agents, bookings, credits and integrations. Tap a suggested question or type your own, for example **Who are my hottest leads right now?** (it lists your most recent qualified calls).

## Ask it to make a change

On Growth plans, Copilot can also act for you. It prepares the action and shows a **Needs your confirmation** card — nothing changes until you click **Confirm**. **Cancel** leaves everything as it is.

Copilot can:

- Pause an agent or put it live
- Change an agent's name, language or welcome message
- Book a callback or an appointment
- Choose which integrations an agent sends leads to
- Route a phone number to an agent (admins)

<Tip>
  Click **Larger** for a bigger panel, and use the thumbs or **Report** under a reply to tell us how it did.
</Tip>
