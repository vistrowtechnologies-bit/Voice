## What Vistrow Voice does

Vistrow Voice runs AI voice agents that answer and place phone calls, staff a website call widget, and qualify leads — in Hindi, English, and 9 other Indian languages, including natural mid-sentence code-switching (Hinglish and beyond).

## Built for how India actually calls

- **11 languages** — Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia — with mid-call language switching when a caller actually switches.
- **Real turn-taking** — configurable interruption sensitivity and end-of-call silence handling, tuned per agent.
- **Compliance built in** — TRAI calling-window enforcement, Do-Not-Call registry scrubbing, and consent logging, on by default.
